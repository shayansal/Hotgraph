# Redaction And Legal Hold Runbook

Use for right-to-be-forgotten, redaction, or legal hold workflows.

1. Record the principal, tenant, source IDs, memory IDs, and legal basis.
2. Apply redaction events instead of deleting audit history.
3. Verify source fetches, evidence packs, summaries, contradictions, paths, and caches do not expose redacted content.
4. Rebuild or invalidate derived summaries and context packs.
5. Confirm legal-hold records block destructive deletion.
6. Store verification output in the release or incident evidence folder.
